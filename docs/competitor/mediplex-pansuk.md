# Competitor: Mediplex (Thailand) / "ปันสุข" (Pansook)

> Research date: 2026-10-13. Desk research only (public web). Every factual claim cites a source in
> the **Sources** section as `[S#]`. Items marked **UNVERIFIED** are inferences or come from
> hearsay, not from a public document. Confirm them in the field before using in a bid.

## TL;DR

- **"ปันสุข" does not show up publicly as a software product.** What does exist is **บริษัท ปัญญ์สุข เมดิคัล จำกัด
  (PANSOOK MEDICAL CO., LTD.)** [S3][S4][S5], an operating-room **equipment** distributor that is a sister company of
  **บริษัท เมดดิเพล็กซ์ (ไทยแลนด์) จำกัด (Mediplex Thailand)**. The two share an office in Bang Phli, Samut Prakan [S1][S2][S6].
  The "Mediplex ปันสุข" that comes up in tenders is most likely this **Mediplex + Pansook Medical group**, with
  "ปันสุข" a phonetic spelling of "ปัญญ์สุข". **UNVERIFIED:** please confirm with the person who reported the name.
- **Mediplex is a medical-device distributor, not a software house.** Its site lists Anesthesia, Ventilator,
  Patient Monitor, Infusion and Ultrasound systems [S6][S7]. The anesthesia models it lists (A7 / A8 / A9 / WATO EX-65/35/20,
  "AnaeSight") are **Mindray** product names [S7][S9]. The site itself never names Mindray, and no public
  source confirms that Mediplex is Mindray's exclusive Thai distributor (**UNVERIFIED**).
- **There is one weak sign of software:** Mediplex advertised an "Application Support" job at an "affiliated company,
  Chiang Mai branch" to support a "Medical system" for hospital users. Hospital/EMR experience was preferred [S8].
  No product name, modules or reference sites are public.
- **For FLORA, the threat is a device-led bundle.** Expect anesthesia machines, monitors and pumps sold together, with
  Mindray's own connectivity (eGateway / BeneLink / BeneVision CMS) [S10][S11] and possibly a local charting app
  bundled in or given away. This is not a like-for-like AIMS/CIS competitor.

## 2. Product capabilities

### 2.1 What is publicly confirmed (hardware)

- **Anesthesia:** A7, A8, A9, WATO EX-65 / EX-35 / EX-20, and "AnaeSight", described as an integrated
  combined IV-inhalational anesthesia solution that connects anesthesia machine, monitor and pumps [S7]. Mindray's own
  press release describes A7/A5 and AnaeSight with the eMAC indicator [S9].
- **Ventilators, patient monitors, infusion, ultrasound:** category pages only, no detail [S6].
- **OR room equipment (via Pansook):** tables, lights, endoscopy camera, pendants, C-arm/fluoroscopy [S4].

### 2.2 Software / information system ("ปันสุข" system?)

- **No public product page, brochure, manual, academic paper or tender document** describes a software system
  named ปันสุข / Pansuk / Pansook / Punsook from Mediplex. See the search log in section 6.
- **Indirect evidence:** a Mediplex job ad for **Application Support** at an affiliated company's Chiang Mai branch.
  The role supports users of the company's "Medical system" via phone, LINE, email and ticket; does first-level
  troubleshooting and escalates to developers; and handles installation, configuration and basic testing. It requires
  shift and holiday work and prefers Hospital, Healthcare or EMR experience [S8]. This suggests the group (or an affiliate)
  **supports a deployed clinical software product with an in-house or partner dev team**. The product name, scope
  (AIMS? ICU charting? nursing documentation?) and owner are **UNVERIFIED**.
- **Likely connectivity stack (inference):** if Mediplex sells Mindray, the device-to-HIS path it can offer out of
  the box is Mindray **eGateway** (HL7 / IHE PCD, bidirectional vitals, alarms, ADT pull), **BeneLink** (third-party
  vents and pumps into Mindray monitors) and **BeneVision CMS/DMS / M-Connect** central monitoring [S10][S11]. These are
  monitor-centric and vendor-locked. Third-party devices only come in through a Mindray monitor.

## 5. Strengths, weaknesses, FLORA positioning

### 5.1 Likely strengths (inferred from the business model)

- **Device-led bundle:** they can include charting or connectivity "free" with anesthesia machines and monitors, which is
  attractive when a hospital buys equipment and software from one budget line.
- **Local field service:** nationwide installation and service claims [S6], plus regional reps and application support [S1][S8].
- **Room-level offer** with Pansook (table, lights, pendants, C-arm) [S4], so they can bid a whole OR renovation package.
- **Mindray hardware is common and price-competitive in Thai public hospitals** (FLORA README seed list).

### 5.2 Likely weaknesses

- **Vendor lock-in:** connectivity runs through Mindray monitors and eGateway [S10][S11]. Mixed fleets (Dräger,
  GE, Philips, Nihon Kohden) are second-class.
- **No public evidence of a mature AIMS/CIS product:** no references, papers, brochures or manuals. Clinical depth
  (pre-op, intra-op record, PACU, ICU flowsheet, drug and fluid balance, scores) is unproven.
- **A distributor, not a software company:** roadmap, customization speed and hospital self-authoring are probably limited.
  **UNVERIFIED.**
- **Small company:** an estimated 11-50 staff [S13, unverified], which is a risk for multi-site tier-1 rollouts and 24x7 SLA.

### 5.3 FLORA positioning

| Segment | Expected Mediplex play | FLORA counter |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Tier 1** (university, MOPH regional/central) | Unlikely to win a standalone AIMS/ICU CIS bid without references; may appear as **device vendor** in the same project | Push TOR toward **vendor-neutral device integration** (multi-brand monitors, anesthesia machines, pumps through Gateway), **HIS bi-directional integration**, **multi-ward Canopy and enterprise reporting**, **audit, PDPA and PHI masking**, and **Form/Report/Workflow Studio self-authoring**. Ask for named Thai reference sites with live HIS interfaces. **Partner option:** FLORA Gateway can ingest Mindray devices, so position FLORA as the software layer on top of Mediplex-supplied hardware instead of fighting them. |
| **Tier 2** (community, private, smaller provincial) | **Bundled "free" software** with anesthesia machine or monitor purchases; low price; local service | Compete on **total value**: works with the hospital's existing mixed fleet (no rip-and-replace), Thai UI, fast deployment (Leaf + Canopy appliance), and Thai-standard forms out of the box. Offer a **light package** and per-OR pricing. Raise lock-in in the TOR: "รองรับการเชื่อมต่อเครื่องมือแพทย์หลายยี่ห้อ" and "ข้อมูลส่งออก/ส่งต่อได้ตามมาตรฐาน HL7/FHIR". |

**Suggested TOR clauses that favour FLORA** (stay factual and non-exclusionary):

- Connects to at least N brands of patient monitor and anesthesia machine without changing the monitors.
- Bidirectional HIS integration (ADT, orders, results, charge capture) with named live Thai sites.
- Hospital staff can build and edit forms and reports themselves without vendor code changes.
- Offline-tolerant bedside operation and central server with full audit trail.
